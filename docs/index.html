Questions

Short answers. The code is on GitHub if you want the long ones.

Is it free?

Yes. No account, no ads, no subscription. Ony is open source under the MIT license.

Why do I have to switch on links in Settings?

Since Android 12, only you can hand another company's links, Spotify's or YouTube's, to a different app. It keeps random apps from hijacking links. Ony can't do it for you, so it shows you exactly which switches to turn on.

Does it work in my country?

Yes. Ony searches your music app's catalogue for the country your phone is in, so you get the version your store actually has. If your store doesn't carry a song, Ony opens the original link instead.

What does Ony collect?

Nothing. There's no Ony server. To find a song, your phone asks Apple's and YouTube Music's public search and reads Spotify's public song pages directly. Your app choice stays on your phone.

I use Spotify. Does it work for me?

Yes, with one extra tap. Spotify has no free search Ony can use, so links open Spotify's search for the song, usually with the song at the top.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Ony is Android only, version 8 and up.
